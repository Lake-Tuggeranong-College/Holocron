# Student Guide: Task 2.2 Showcase Video
**Qualification:** ICT30120 Certificate III in Information Technology  
**Unit of Competency:** ICTICT215 Operate digital media technology packages  
**Task Type:** Practical / Written Demonstration & Digital Media Package Production  
**Submission Platform:** Google Classroom  

---

## 1. Overview & Purpose
In this assessment, you will create a **2-minute showcase video** ($\pm 10\%$, i.e., 1:48 to 2:12) that demonstrates your final project, analyses how it meets its original goals and user requirements, and highlights its technical features. 

You are required to demonstrate competencies in digital media production, including software navigation, media asset management, voiceover recording or AI narration, feedback incorporation, and proper workplace health and safety (WHS) practices.

---

## 2. Key Requirements Checklist

### A. Pre-Production & Safety (Observational Assessment)
Before starting video editing or recording:
* [ ] **Workstation Setup:** Ensure your desk, monitor, chair, and peripherals meet ergonomic requirements (correct posture, screen distance, lighting).
* [ ] **Equipment Inspection:** Conduct a WHS check on hardware (cables, headsets, monitors). Report any faulty equipment immediately to your instructor.
* [ ] **Software Readiness:** Select and install your capturing/editing tools and verify access to help/documentation resources.

### B. Media & Asset Collection
* [ ] Gather high-quality screen recordings, screenshots, and visual evidence demonstrating your project in action.
* [ ] Store and manage files using standard naming conventions and structured folder hierarchies.
* [ ] Prepare a script or commentary text explaining technical specifications, features, and alignment with user requirements.

### C. Video Production & Content Structure
Your 2-minute video **must** contain:
1. **Introduction & Project Aims:** Brief overview of the project and its intended goals.
2. **Live Demonstration:** High-quality visual evidence (screen recordings/captures) showing the project in its final working state.
3. **Technical Analysis:** Voiced narration (recorded voice or AI generated) detailing how specific features satisfy user needs and technical specifications.
4. **Conclusion:** Summary of project outcomes.

### D. Technical & Formatting Specifications
* [ ] **Duration:** 1:48 to 2:12 (2 minutes $\pm 10\%$).
* [ ] **Audio:** Clear narration balanced properly with background audio/effects (no distortion or hard-to-hear dialogue).
* [ ] **Export Format:** Export as MP4 format.
* [ ] **File Naming Convention:** `Shopfront Showcase.mp4`

## 3. Recommended Free Software & Learning Resources

> [!important] You may use any tools you wish to capture or edit the audio and video. These are just suggestions.

You are encouraged to use open-source or free video editing and screen recording tools. Below are the primary recommended tools along with official tutorials and user guides:

### 📹 Screen Recording & Media Capture
* **OBS Studio (Open Broadcaster Software)**
  * **Description:** Free, open-source software for screen recording and live streaming. Excellent for capturing high-definition screen demonstrations and audio commentary.
  * **Download:** [obsproject.com](https://obsproject.com/)
  * **Learning Resources:**
    * [OBS Studio Quickstart Guide](https://obsproject.com/wiki/OBS-Studio-Quickstart)
    * [OBS Studio Official Forum Guides](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/)

### ✂️ Video Editing & Assembly
* **Kdenlive**
  * **Description:** A powerful, free, open-source non-linear video editor available on Windows, Linux, and macOS. Ideal for timeline trimming, voiceover alignment, and exporting to `.mp4`.
  * **Download:** [kdenlive.org](https://kdenlive.org/)
  * **Learning Resources:**
    * [Kdenlive Official Documentation & Quick Start](https://docs.kdenlive.org/en/user_interface/quick_start.html)
    * [Kdenlive Video Tutorials Collection](https://kdenlive.org/en/toolbox/)

* **Alternative Free Tools:**
  * **CapCut (Desktop Edition):** Easy-to-use desktop editor with built-in text-to-speech AI voiceover features. ([capcut.com](https://www.capcut.com/))
  * **DaVinci Resolve (Free Version):** Industry-standard editing software for high-end colour grading and audio mixing. ([blackmagicdesign.com](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve))

See [[Video Production]] for more details.

## 4. Workflow & Submission Steps

### Step 1: Ergonomic Check & Observation
Have your teacher perform an observational assessment while you configure your workstation and set up your video editing software project.

### Step 2: Create & Submit Draft Video
1. Produce your draft showcase video adhering to all content and length requirements.
2. Export as a draft video file and upload to the designated Google Classroom assignment.
3. Explicitly request constructive feedback from your teacher/instructor on audio, visual layout, and content clarity.

### Step 3: Feedback & Final Editing
1. Review teacher feedback carefully.
2. Adjust timeline cuts, audio levels, overlay screens, or narrative scripts as requested.
3. Re-export the video following final render settings.

### Step 4: Final Submission
* Upload your final version named `Shopfront Showcase.mp4` to Google Classroom prior to the due date.

## 5. Foundation Skills & Evaluation
Your work will be assessed against both technical performance criteria and foundation skills:
* **Numeracy:** Time calculations, audio output levels, graphic aspect ratios, and export file sizes.
* **Oral Communication & Writing:** Clear technical language, appropriate vocabulary, syntax, and voice presentation.
* **Self-Management & WHS:** Adherence to safe physical work setup and legal/regulatory requirements for digital media.
