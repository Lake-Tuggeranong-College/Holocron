# BSBCRT301
## Critical & Creative Thinking Design Sprint
### Collaborative Website Layout & Feature Concept

---

## Workshop Overview

* **Format:** 2 Sessions x 2 Hours (4 Hours Total)
* **Goal:** Design and agree on a **Final Class Website Concept**
* **Method:** Design Sprint (Visual Sketching + Discussion + Reflection)

--

## Progression Model

* **Week 1: Small Group Exploration (120 Mins Total)**
  * Phase 1: Problem Definition & Research (20 Mins)
  * Phase 2: Creative Layout Sprint (35 Mins)
  * Phase 2A: Overcoming Blockers (15 Mins)
  * Phase 2B: Future Tech Realities (20 Mins)
  * Phase 3: Small Group Layout Merging (30 Mins)

* **Week 2: Whole Class Decision (120 Mins Total)**
  * Phase 4: Pitches, Multi-Perspective Q&A & Boundaries (50 Mins)
  * Phase 5: Whole Class Consensus & Wireframe (40 Mins)
  * Phase 6: Individual Reflection & Wrap-Up (30 Mins)

---

# WEEK 1
## Small Group Exploration (120 Mins Total)

---

## Phase 1: Problem Definition & Research
### Duration: 20 Minutes

* **Task 1: Search Diverse Sources (10 Mins)**
  * Explore at least **2 information sources** outside standard templates.
  * *Examples:* UX blogs, competitor sites, architectural designs, nature patterns.
* **Task 2: Define the Core Problem (10 Mins)**
  * What primary challenge must your website layout solve for users?
  * Write down a single, clear problem statement.

---

## Phase 2: Creative Layout Sprint
### Duration: 35 Minutes

* **Connecting the Unconnected (10 Mins)**
  * Pick 2 non-digital mechanics or shapes (e.g. vending machines, spiral staircases, board games).
  * Adapt these mechanics into website UI features.

--

## Crazy Eights / SCAMPER Sprint
### Duration: 25 Minutes

* **8 Minutes, 8 Quick Layout Ideas**
  * Sketch fast without self-censorship.
* **Produce Two Final Variations (17 Mins):**
  * **Variation A:** Refined, conventional, clean layout.
  * **Variation B:** High-risk, bold, unconventional layout.

---

## Phase 2A: Overcoming Creative Blockers
### Duration: 15 Minutes

* **Identify Mental Blockers (5 Mins)**
  * What made you hesitate during sketching?
  * *Examples:* Fear of making unworkable designs, reliance on standard templates, self-censorship.
* **Breakthrough Strategy (10 Mins)**
  * Share 1 blocker with your group.
  * Test a solution (e.g., "What if there is no top nav bar?", "What if navigation is vertical only?").

---

## Phase 2B: Exploring Future Realities
### Duration: 20 Minutes

* **Project 10 Years Ahead (5 Mins)**
  * Imagine website interaction beyond screens and traditional browsers.
* **Futuristic UI Features (15 Mins)**
  * Eye-tracking navigation
  * Spatial AR expansion
  * Adaptive AI layout shifting
* **Action:** Annotate or sketch 1 futuristic feature on your concept sheet.

---

## Phase 3: Small Group Layout Merging
### Duration: 30 Minutes

* **Synthesise Your Ideas (15 Mins)**
  * Compare individual Variation A and B sketches.
  * Select and merge the best visual elements.
* **Output: Small Group Hybrid Layout Sketch (15 Mins)**
  * Clearly annotate 3–4 key interactive features.

---

## Week 1 Submission Checkpoint
### End of Session 1

* **Deliverable 1 (Visual Submission):**
  * 1 page containing Crazy Eights / SCAMPER variations and your Small Group Hybrid Sketch.
* **Assessor Practical Observation:**
  * Evaluates research sources, problem definition, experimentation, blockers, future tech, and unconnected inspiration.

---

# WEEK 2
## Whole Class Decision (120 Mins Total)

---

## Phase 4: Pitch & Multi-Perspective Session
### Duration: 50 Minutes

* **Pitching Small Group Concepts (20 Mins)**
  * Each group presents their Hybrid Layout Sketch to the class (approx. 5 mins per group).
* **Audience Role: Multi-Perspective Questions (30 Mins)**
  * Ask targeted questions using 3 distinct lenses:
    * **End-User / Accessibility Lens**
    * **Client / Business Lens**
    * **Disruptive / Creative Lens**

---

## Challenging Preconceptions & Constraints
### Included in Phase 4 Discussion

* **Preconceptions vs. Real Constraints**
  * Challenge 1 conventional web layout rule (e.g., "Websites must have a top header menu").
  * Separate assumptions from real technical/accessibility rules (e.g., contrast ratios, touch targets, screen boundaries).
* **Discussing Creative Habits**
  * How do we prevent falling back into default patterns?
  * How do we make room for experimentation in tight project deadlines?

---

## Phase 5: Whole Class Consensus
### Duration: 40 Minutes

* **Collaborative Wireframe Creation (20 Mins)**
  * Negotiate trade-offs between group pitches.
  * Resolve layout conflicts through discussion.
  * Involve everyone in refining the user flow.
* **Finalizing Class Concept (20 Mins)**
  * Agree on and draw one single **Final Class Website Concept Wireframe**.

---

## Phase 6: Individual Reflection
### Duration: 30 Minutes

* **Answer 3 Brief Reflection Questions (20 Mins):**
  1. **Thought Pattern Shifts:** What default assumptions about web design did you unlearn?
  2. **Self-Assessment:** Where did you excel in the creative process, and where can you grow?
  3. **Future Real-World Application:** How will you apply these techniques in future work or study?
* **Attach/Draw & Submit (10 Mins):** Include a visual sketch of the agreed Final Class Layout Wireframe alongside your sheet.

---

## Week 2 Submission Checkpoint
### End of Session 2

* **Deliverable 2 (Written & Visual Submission):**
  * Completed Individual Reflection Sheet + Final Class Wireframe Sketch.
* **Assessor Practical Observation:**
  * Evaluates multi-perspective questions, boundary testing, assumption interrogation, and consensus building.

---

# Summary & Wrap-Up

* **Key Takeaway:** Creative thinking is a structured skill, not random inspiration.
* **Next Steps:** Submit your reflection sheet to complete the assessment.
