# Task 2.2: Showcase Video
### ICTICT215 - Operate Digital Media Technology Packages
**Student Brief & Presentation**

---

## Overview & Objective

* **Goal:** Create a **2-minute showcase video** ($\pm 10\%$, i.e. 1:48 to 2:12).
* **Purpose:** Demonstrate project functionality and analyse how technical features meet user requirements.
* **Format:** Voiced narration (recorded or AI-generated) over screen captures & video footage.
* **Submission:** Final file uploaded as `Shopfront Showcase.mp4` on Google Classroom.

---

## Assessment Components

1. **Observational Assessment:**
   * Ergonomic workstation setup
   * Safe handling & inspection of digital hardware
   * Software environment setup
2. **Draft Submission & Feedback:**
   * Submit draft video for teacher critique
   * Incorporate feedback into final render
3. **Final Showcase Video Submission**

---

## Key Video Content Requirements

* **Introduction:** Brief description of stated project aims.
* **Demonstration:** Visual evidence of the project operating in its final state.
* **Analysis:** Detailed technical narrative explaining how design features satisfy user needs.
* **Audio Quality:** Clear voiceover commentary properly balanced against background audio.

---

### ✂️ Video Editing: **Kdenlive**
* Open-source non-linear video editor for Windows, Mac & Linux.
* Ideal for timeline editing, audio balancing, and MP4 rendering.
* [Download Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org/)
* [Kdenlive Official User Guide](https://docs.kdenlive.org/en/user_interface/quick_start.html)

## Production Workflow

1. **Safety & Ergonomics Check** (Instructor Observation)
2. **Collect Evidence:** Screenshots, screen recordings, media files
3. **Draft Production:** Edit timeline & record voiceover
4. **Draft Submission:** Upload to Google Classroom & request feedback
5. **Refinement:** Incorporate suggestions & re-export
6. **Final Submission:** `Shopfront Showcase.mp4`

---

## Foundation Skills Assessed

* **Numeracy:** Managing track duration, frame dimensions, audio decibels & file size.
* **Communication:** Technical vocabulary, syntax, and engaging audio delivery.
* **Self-Management:** Following WHS rules, legal compliance, and adhering to file-naming standards.
